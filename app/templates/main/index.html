System Design

Enterprise RAG Architecture

Unlike standard generative chatbots that hallucinate policies, Smart College Assistant AI utilizes a strict Retrieval-Augmented Generation pipeline. Official college documents are parsed, chunked, and embedded into a high-performance vector store.

  • Hallucination Defense: The AI model is strictly prohibited from inventing college policies. If an answer is not in the knowledge base, it cleanly directs the student to the appropriate college office.
  • Adaptive Feedback Loop: Students can report missing or incorrect answers. Administrators verify corrections and instantly index them as verified FAQs without retraining the foundation model.
  • Role-Based Access Control: Strict backend permission checks across Student, Faculty, and Administrator roles with session security.
